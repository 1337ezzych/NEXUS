# App Store preparation checklist

- Use an original name, icon, interface and marketing copy. Do not imply that NEXUS is an official Telegram app or endorsed by Telegram.
- Review Apple's current App Review Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Provide a privacy policy and accurate App Privacy disclosures.
- Protect account/session data and explain what data is processed and why.
- For user-generated content, implement appropriate moderation/reporting/blocking and a support contact.
- Test crashes, network loss, account logout, data deletion and accessibility.
- Do not download and execute arbitrary native plugin binaries. Prefer a limited, documented extension system (themes, layouts, safe settings and capabilities) with clear permissions.
- App Store enrollment and legal agreements must be completed by an eligible account holder. A minor may need a parent or guardian to handle enrollment and agreements.
- TDLib inclusion alone does not guarantee App Store approval; the complete app is reviewed.
